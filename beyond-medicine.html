Beyond medicine

The interests that keep a future surgeon sharp outside the hospital, and remind me why the work is worth it.
01 · CAR PHOTOGRAPHY
‹
›
Captured rides
Cars were my thing long before I could legally drive one. I started photographing them at meets around Lublin, killing an evening in a car park with friends, waiting for the light to catch a body line just right, or chasing a clean frame of someone's build under the sodium lamps. Half of it is patience and half is just loving the machines. Honestly, it's the same slow, careful eye I try to bring into the operating theatre.
02 · MOUNTAIN SUMMITING
‹
›
Scaling heights
Nothing resets my head like a hard climb. Some of my best trips have been in the dead of winter, pitching a tent in the snow, boiling water on a tiny stove, waking up to a frozen white world with the tent glowing red in the dark. It's cold, it's uncomfortable, and I love every minute of it. Long ascents ask for exactly what surgery will: preparation, pacing, and staying calm when your body wants to quit.
03 · SAILING
‹
›
Freedom on open water
I learned to sail on the Masurian lakes and I've been hooked ever since. These days I get to pass it on, crewing and helping teach younger sailors how to read the wind. There's a real freedom to it: no engine, no schedule, just you working with conditions you can't control, only cooperate with. Some evenings the water goes gold at sunset and you honestly forget you have anywhere else to be.
04 · WEIGHTS & BOXING
Strength, trained daily
The gym is my one non-negotiable. Lifting and boxing are how I stay sharp and burn off stress. There's something honest about it, you either put the work in or you don't, and the numbers don't lie. Boxing especially keeps me humble and quick on my feet. It's the same discipline I carry into everything else: show up, do the reps, and get a little bit better every single day.
